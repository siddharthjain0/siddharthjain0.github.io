Research

Mar 2026–

Quantum networks from spin models

University of Pittsburgh · Advisors: Zhehao Dai, Kaushik Seshadreesan

Investigating physical lattice models — specifically the random transverse-field Ising model — as a testbed for real-world quantum networks. I have characterized the multipartite entanglement structure of the emergent quantum network, and am currently developing methods to optimally exploit these entanglement resources by achieving network topologies suited to tasks such as quantum communication and routing, informed by the underlying spin model.

May–Nov 2025

Hofstadter–Hubbard model

University of Pittsburgh · Advisor: Zhehao Dai

Studied the fractional quantum Hall effect and Chern–Simons theory. Implemented a slave-rotor (parton) mean-field treatment of the triangular-lattice Hofstadter–Hubbard model, built a simulation of spinon-excitation dynamics, and probed the excitation's edge behavior on the lattice in Python.

Aug 2024–May 2025

Floquet analysis for the SNAIL

University of Pittsburgh · Advisor: Roger Mong

Derived the effective Floquet Hamiltonian of a driven anharmonic oscillator with cubic nonlinearity — the reduced model of a driven SNAIL circuit mode — via a Magnus expansion, and built a numerical time-evolution routine in Python to validate the derivation.

Jan–Jul 2022

Diffusion under stochastic resetting

Master's research project · IMSc Chennai · Advisor: Arnab Pal

Studied escape properties of a diffusing particle in conical-tube geometry under a stochastic resetting strategy. Modified the Fick–Jacobs equation to include resetting and solved it analytically for the statistical quantities of the process.

Escape times turn out to depend strongly on the resetting rate and the initial position. Counter-intuitively, hindering the process at a finite rate can significantly speed up escape; I identified when this occurs and the optimal reset rate minimizing escape time.
