Investigating physical lattice models — specifically the random
transverse-field Ising model (RTIM) — as a testbed for real-world quantum
networks. I have characterized the multipartite entanglement structure of the
emergent quantum network, and am currently developing methods to optimally
exploit these entanglement resources by achieving network topologies suited to
tasks such as quantum communication and routing, informed by the underlying
spin model.

All figures below are averaged over 50 disorder realizations.
