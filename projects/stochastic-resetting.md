Counter-intuitively, hindering a diffusing particle at a finite rate can make
it escape *faster*. This project worked out when that happens, and what the
optimal reset rate is.

I studied the escape properties of a diffusing particle in a conical-tube
geometry under a stochastic resetting strategy, modifying the Fick–Jacobs
equation to include resetting and solving it analytically for the statistical
quantities of the process. Escape times depend strongly on both the resetting
rate and the initial position; I identified the conditions under which
resetting speeds up escape and the reset rate that minimises escape time.

Published in the *Journal of Chemical Physics* — see
[the paper](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0135249) or
[arXiv:2211.07904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.07904).
